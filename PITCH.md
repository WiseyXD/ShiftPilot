## Slide 5 — How it works (in 5 steps)

**1. Weekly availability ask** (auto) — every employee gets an email link to confirm or change their availability for next week. Pre-filled with their usual pattern.

**2. AI builds the schedule** — using contracted hours, roles required per shift, fairness across the team, and time-off requests.

**3. You approve** — one click. Schedule goes out.

**4. Each employee gets one email** with all their shifts. **Accept / Decline / Request a swap / Add to Calendar** — every action is one click. No app to install.

**5. When something changes** — sick call, decline, swap request — **ShiftPilot ranks your team by who should cover** (volunteers first, then anyone under their contracted hours, then by fairness) and emails them automatically until someone says yes. You only hear about it if all candidates exhausted.

## Slide 7 — The replacement engine (live demo moment)

When Maria calls in sick at 7am for the 9am shift, here's what happens **without any input from you**:

```
7:00am   Maria taps "Report Sick" in her shift email
7:00am   ShiftPilot ranks the rest of the team:
            ① Tom — volunteered for extra hours this week
            ② Sarah — under her contracted minimum
            ③ Jake — fairly distributed
7:00am   Email to Tom: "Can you cover Maria's 9am shift?"
7:14am   Tom clicks "Yes, I'll take it"
7:14am   Shift is reassigned. Tom gets a confirmation +
         Google Calendar invite. You get a notification.
```

**14 minutes. No phone calls. No begging. No "I'll see who's free."**

## Speaker notes (not a slide)

When pitching live, lead with **Slide 7 (the replacement engine demo)** — that's the "aha" moment. Skip backwards to the problem framing if they're not already sold. Always close with **Slide 11 (ROI)** — restaurant operators are price-sensitive but ROI-rational.

**Common objections & responses:**
- *"My team won't use it"* → "There's nothing for them to use. They get an email, they click. That's it. No app."
- *"What if the AI screws up the schedule?"* → "You approve every schedule before it goes out. The AI just does the work; you sign off."
- *"What if I have a complex roster the AI can't handle?"* → "Manual editing is one click in the dashboard. The AI gives you a starting point that's 95% right; you spend 2 minutes adjusting."
- *"How is this different from Deputy / 7shifts?"* → "Those tools help you *click* less. ShiftPilot helps you *think* less. We do the actual work."
